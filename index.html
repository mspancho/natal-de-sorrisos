Who's behind this

Casa de Apoio Aura

A support house in Belo Horizonte, Brazil. Since 1998, Casa Aura has welcomed children and teens up to 17 who travel from across the country for cancer treatment, blood disorders, and transplants, together with the family members who stay by their side. Around 70 volunteers keep the house playful, with school support, outings, and celebrations like this one.

aura.org.br

Umeed at Brown University

Umeed means hope in Hindi and Urdu. The student group raises funds and awareness for cancer patients in low- and middle-income countries, through benefit concerts, a fun run, and a day of giving, and sends the proceeds to partner organizations that cover treatment and hospital costs. This Christmas, Umeed collects the donations and transfers the full amount to Casa Aura.

brownumeed.org @brown_umeed

How a gift reaches a child

  1. Pick an ornament

    Each one is a real gift with a price and an age range.

  2. Send the amount to Brown Umeed

    Venmo @brown_umeed or Zelle 786-266-2630. Put the gift number in the note.

  3. Confirm in the short form

    Tell us which gift you covered and whether you want to stay anonymous or share your name and a message with the family. The ornament lights up once you confirm.

  4. Casa Aura does the shopping

    Umeed transfers the money to Casa Aura. The team in Belo Horizonte buys the gift, wraps the present, and hands the present to the child at the Christmas celebration.

Support treatment all year

Beyond Christmas, Brown Umeed covers treatment and hospital costs for low-income cancer patients in Brazil and India through partner organizations. A general donation goes wherever the need is greatest.
